A parish and a skete

Our community is both a parish church and a small monastic skete, set on a quiet residential street in Fridley, just north of Minneapolis. The same little church that shelters the daily prayer of the brotherhood is the church where the parish gathers each Sunday.

We belong to the Russian Orthodox Church Outside Russia, in the Diocese of Chicago and Mid-America.

About our parish →

Never been to an Orthodox service?

You do not need to know anything in advance, and you do not need to do anything but stand, listen and pray. There is no admission, no registration, and no one will single you out. Come a few minutes early, light a candle if you wish, and simply pray with us.

Parish News

Our new bishop, Vladyka Spyridon

His Grace Bishop Spyridon (Gusakov), formerly of Toronto, has been appointed by the Council of Bishops of the Russian Orthodox Church Outside Russia to the See of Chicago and Mid-America, which had been widowed since the repose of Archbishop Peter (Loukianoff) on November 8, 2024.

Our patronal feast: St. Seraphim of Sarov

Each year on August 1 we keep the feast of the Glorification of St. Seraphim of Sarov, the patronal feast of our skete, with the Vigil the evening before and the Divine Liturgy in the morning.
