The Church Year

Beyond the weekly cycle, the greater feasts are served with a Vigil on the eve and the Divine Liturgy on the morning of the feast. The dates below follow the calendar our parish keeps; please confirm times in the bulletin, since a feast falling on a weekday is sometimes served at a different hour.

Our patronal feast

August 1 — the Glorification of St. Seraphim of Sarov, the patron of our skete. This is the great day of our year, kept with the Vigil on the evening of July 31 and the Divine Liturgy on the morning of August 1, followed by a festal meal.

Pascha and Holy Week

Holy Week and Pascha have their own schedule, printed and distributed well in advance. The Paschal services begin late on Holy Saturday night and continue into the small hours of Pascha morning, and are followed by the blessing of the Paschal foods.

Fasting seasons

The Church keeps four fasting seasons — the Nativity Fast, Great Lent, the Apostles' Fast and the Dormition Fast — as well as most Wednesdays and Fridays through the year. If you are new to fasting, speak with the priest rather than taking it on all at once.

Baptisms, Weddings & Memorials

Baptism

Baptisms are arranged with the rector. Please contact us well in advance so that the parents and godparents can be prepared.

Marriage

Couples wishing to be married in the church should speak with the rector before setting a date. Weddings are not celebrated during the fasting seasons.

Memorial services

A panikhida may be served for the departed on request. Names for commemoration at the Liturgy may be given to the priest before the service.

Confession & house blessings

Confessions are heard after the Saturday Vigil and before the Sunday Liturgy, or by arrangement. House blessings and other needs can be arranged by telephone.
