Readings & Homilies

Readings gathered for this parish over many years, each one given here in both English and Russian: on the feasts of the Church, on preparing for confession and communion, and on how to live as a Christian.

These pieces were written and gathered over many years for the people of this parish. Some are homilies preached at the Liturgy; others are practical instructions of the kind that are easier to read at home than to remember from the ambo — how to prepare for confession, how to venerate an icon, how to conduct oneself in church. Where a reading came from another author or publication, the credit is given at the foot of the page.
