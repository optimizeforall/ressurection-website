Support Our Church

Dear parishioners and friends! Our church and monastery are in need of financial assistance. If you are able to help, may God bless you.

A small parish and skete has no endowment and no diocesan subsidy that covers the ordinary costs of keeping a church open: heat, insurance, repairs, the upkeep of the grounds, and the life of the brotherhood. What we have is given by the people who pray here, and by friends at a distance who remember us.

Ways to Give

Online, by PayPal or card

You may give a one-time gift through PayPal, using a PayPal account or any major credit card. You do not need a PayPal account.

By check

Checks may be made payable to Church of the Resurrection of Christ Inc, and either handed to us in person at the church or mailed to:

Church of the Resurrection of Christ
1201 Hathaway Lane NE
Fridley, MN 55432

We are a tax-exempt religious organization; our tax-exempt number can be provided on request.

In church

A candle stand near the entrance is the ordinary place for offerings.

A note of thanks

If you have given already, you have our gratitude. If you have questions about a gift — including memorial gifts in honour of the departed — please write to rusmnch@msn.com or telephone (763) 744-8601. Commemoration of the departed explains how names are submitted.

All ye Saints, pray to God for us!
